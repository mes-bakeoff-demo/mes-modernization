# Professional Services Contract and Work Order Template

> Adapted from [SI Delivery Starter Kit](https://si-delivery.com/starter-kit/)

## Contract Structure

This is a **container contract** model:

- **Master Contract:** 1-year term, establishes capacity
- **Work Orders:** Discrete authorizations for specific Backlog Items
- **No guaranteed volume:** Contract doesn't guarantee any Work Orders

---

## Key Terms

### Financial Structure

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Monthly NTE** | Maximum compensation per calendar month |
| **Annual Contract Maximum** | Monthly NTE × 12 |
| **Retainage** | Withheld until Definition of Done accepted |

### Delivery Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Iteration** | One calendar month |
| **Backlog Item** | Slice or Layer from published backlog |
| **Definition of Done** | Acceptance criteria for Backlog Item |
| **Value Report** | Monthly deliverable documenting outcomes and costs |

---

## Contractor Responsibilities

1. **Perform services** per Work Order and Definition of Done
2. **Provide qualified personnel** with appropriate skills
3. **Maintain delivery cadence** per Iteration schedule
4. **Produce required deliverables** including Value Reports
5. **Provide transparency** on progress, costs, risks
6. **Identify dependencies** requiring State action
7. **Comply with policies** (security, privacy, accessibility)

---

## State Responsibilities

### Delivery Manager
- Primary point of contact
- Clarifies Backlog Items and Definition of Done
- Coordinates access to systems and staff
- Makes timely decisions
- Accepts or rejects delivered scope

### Executive Steering Committee
- Escalation authority
- Resolves impediments
- Strategic direction

### Access Provisioning
- Non-production environments
- Required systems and data
- Response to dependency requests within 5 business days

---

## Value Report Contents

Each monthly Iteration, Contractor provides:

1. Delivery progress toward Definition of Done
2. Documentation for integration and reuse
3. Outcome measures demonstrated
4. Dependencies and impediments
5. Observations and recommendations
6. Cost for the month (capped by Monthly NTE)

---

## Acceptance

### Value Report Acceptance
- Authorizes invoice for the month
- Does not constitute Backlog Item acceptance

### Backlog Item Acceptance
- Definition of Done fully satisfied
- Written confirmation from State
- Releases associated retainage

---

## Scaling

The State's mechanism for scaling is **additional contracts**, not expansion of existing contracts.

- High performers may submit proposals for additional Backlog Items
- Each contract = separate delivery team
- Maintains continuous competitive pressure

## Transition and Continuity

- All work products delivered to State
- Documentation sufficient for another vendor to continue
- Knowledge transfer upon request
- No withholding of work product due to disputes
