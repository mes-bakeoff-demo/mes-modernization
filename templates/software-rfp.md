# Challenge-Based Software and Technology RFP Template

> Adapted from [SI Delivery Starter Kit](https://si-delivery.com/starter-kit/)

## Overview

This template establishes a trial-based approach for evaluating and adopting software products. Products are not selected through paper-based evaluation — they're evaluated through demonstrated performance in working implementations.

---

## The 3-Phase Trial Model

### Phase 1: Eligibility for Trial
- Product approved for consideration
- Basic alignment to outcomes verified
- Trial licensing terms acceptable
- **Does not authorize production use**

### Phase 2: Trial Implementation
- Product incorporated into a Services Work Order
- Deployed in non-production environment
- Evaluated through actual delivery
- Multiple products may be evaluated in parallel

### Phase 3: Enterprise Approval
- Security and privacy review
- Enterprise architecture review
- Licensing negotiation
- Production deployment authorization

---

## Key Principles

1. **Products evaluated through real delivery** — not proposals
2. **Trial access provisioned quickly** — to enable experimentation
3. **Enterprise adoption based on demonstrated value** — not vendor claims
4. **Multiple products evaluated concurrently** — continuous competition
5. **Products may be discontinued anytime** — based on performance

---

## Traditional vs. Challenge-Based Software Procurement

| Aspect | Traditional | Challenge-Based |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Timeline | 1-3 years | No fixed timeline; advances based on performance |
| Selection | Written proposals, demos | Integration into Work Orders, demonstrated delivery |
| Decision authority | State evaluators select from docs | Expert integrators propose; State validates through working delivery |
| Risk | High upfront commitment | Phased advancement, ability to discontinue |
| Success criteria | Software implemented | Outcomes improved, integration viable |

## Submission Requirements

### Product Overview
- Product name and description
- Vendor organization and contact
- Intended use cases

### Technical Information
- Architecture description
- Hosting model (SaaS, on-premise, hybrid)
- Integration/API capabilities
- Data export and portability
- Security posture

### Trial Readiness
- Trial environment availability
- Trial terms documentation
- Onboarding timeline
- Support model during trial

### Enterprise Licensing
- Licensing model
- Pricing assumptions
- Constraints on trial use

---

## Conflict of Interest

Software vendors may not offer referral fees, rebates, or other compensation to Professional Services vendors tied to product selection.

All partnerships and commercial relationships must be disclosed.

---

## Trial Terms

- **No direct cost to State** for trial access (expected)
- **Commercially reasonable support** during trial
- **Data ownership** remains with State
- **Data export** required upon discontinuation
