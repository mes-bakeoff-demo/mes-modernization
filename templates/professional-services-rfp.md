## 1. Introduction

This solicitation enables rapid evaluation of vendor capability through delivery of real, narrowly scoped work.

**This is not a traditional requirements-based procurement.** Proposals are evaluated based on ability to deliver defined outcomes, learn quickly, and integrate effectively.

## 2. Container Contract Model

- **1-year contracts** with Monthly Not-to-Exceed (NTE) and Annual Maximum
- **Work Orders** authorize specific Backlog Items
- **Scaling** happens through additional contracts, not bigger contracts
- **Multiple vendors** may work on similar scope simultaneously

## 3. Scope Framework

Work is organized into:

- **Slices:** End-to-end delivery of narrowly scoped outcomes
- **Layers:** Enabling capabilities that support slice delivery

Each Backlog Item includes a **Definition of Done** with acceptance criteria.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

### Pass/Fail Screening
- Agreement to contract terms
- Alignment with current priorities
- Feasibility given State capacity

### Scored Evaluation
- **Prior Delivery Performance** — Demonstrated results under this framework
- **Solution Quality** — Technical soundness, alignment with architecture
- **Delivery Approach** — Iterative value delivery, transparency, adaptability
- **Team Capability** — The 3 A's: Attitude, Aptitude, Abilities
- **Cost** — Value relative to proposed Monthly NTE

## 5. The 3 A's: Team Evaluation

### Attitude (Cultural Alignment)
- Embraces iterative, outcome-oriented delivery
- Values transparency and collaboration
- User-centered thinking
- Genuine commitment to public service

### Aptitude (Learning Agility)
- Quickly understands complex policy contexts
- Navigates ambiguity effectively
- Systems thinking across boundaries
- Learns and improves continuously

### Abilities (Technical Skills)
- Relevant technology expertise
- Domain knowledge
- Appropriate role mix
- Experience matching complexity

## 6. Value Formula

```
Value = Outcomes Achieved ÷ Cost Incurred
```

Not compliance with deliverables. Not hours worked. Actual outcomes relative to investment.

## 7. Submission Requirements

### Slice Proposals
- Company information
- Selected Backlog Item(s)
- Proposed solution and delivery approach
- Dependencies and assumptions
- Proposed team
- Monthly NTE
- Estimated months to deliver

### Layer Proposals
- Same as above, plus:
- Approach to ongoing support/SLAs

## 8. Decision Outcomes

- **Accepted:** Proceed to contract execution
- **Deferred:** Does not meet current criteria (timing, capacity, competition)
- **Rejected:** Non-responsive or unsuitable

Deferred proposals remain eligible. Vendors may revise and resubmit anytime.

## Traditional vs. Challenge-Based

| Aspect | Traditional | Challenge-Based |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Timeline | 12-24 months | 2-4 weeks |
| Commitment | Multi-year, $5M-$100M+ | 1-year, monthly cap |
| Selection | Written proposals | Demonstrated performance |
| Risk | High upfront | Distributed, continuous |
| Competition | Single event | Continuous |
| Failure detection | Late | Early |
